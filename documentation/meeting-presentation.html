PART 1

The Problem & the Biology

What the data is, what PureCLIP does, and why parameters matter.

What is eCLIP?

A method to map where an RNA-binding protein (RBP) touches RNA inside living cells.

UV crosslinkFreeze protein onto the RNA it's bound to.
→
Pull down (IP)Grab the target RBP with a specific antibody.
→
SequenceReverse transcription stops at the crosslink — the read's 5′ end marks the contact.
Two IP replicates — the antibody pulldown for the RBP. Real binding should recur in both.
SMInput control — background signal to subtract. We work from pre-aligned ENCODE BAM files.

Why we care: the crosslink sites tell you the protein's binding map at near-nucleotide resolution.

PureCLIP & why this project exists

  • PureCLIP is an HMM that reads the crosslink-truncation signal and calls crosslink sites and binding regions.
  • Its output is very sensitive to a handful of parameters (bandwidth, merge distance, precision mode…).
  • The right values are dataset-dependent — there is no universal setting. That is the whole problem.

How the System Works

A closed optimization loop around the PureCLIP pipeline.

The Optimization Loop

① ProposeAn optimizer picks a parameter set.
→
② Run PureCLIPCall sites on the eCLIP reads.
→
③ Scorereproducibility · motif · recall → composite.
→
④ DecidePick the next params.
↺
LLM A DeepSeek agent — reasons in natural language from the biology & prior results.
OPTUNA · TPE Bayesian search — models past trials statistically; no reasoning.

Key design: both optimizers share the same pipeline, scoring, and bounds — only the search strategy differs, so the comparison is apples-to-apples.

Inside one iteration — the pipeline

This is exactly the 4-stage stepper you see on the dashboard's Active Run card.

step 1

step 2

step 3

step 4
LLM decide
  • PureCLIP — merge the two IP replicates, call crosslink sites (also per-replicate for reproducibility).
  • Postprocess — standardise each site to the protein's ~9 nt footprint; filter & cluster.
  • Score — compute reproducibility, motif hit-rate, recall → the composite.
  • Decide — optimizer proposes the next parameter set, and the loop repeats.

Fast mode: learn_on_chr21 restricts to chromosome 21 — minutes per iteration instead of hours (used for all batch experiments).

The Vocabulary

Every term on the dashboard, in plain English. (Mirrors the UI "Variables" page.)

The Knobs — what gets tuned

PureCLIP (turns reads → sites) · Post-processing (filters → final site set)

bandwidth_nt20–100
Smoothing window for scanning read coverage. Larger → broader/more sites; smaller → sharper.
merge_distance_nt4–16
How close two sites must be to merge into one.
high_precision_mode0/1
Stricter calling. On → fewer, higher-confidence sites.
use_input_covariate0/1
Use the SMInput control to subtract background.
min_crosslink_events2–6
Min read events to keep a site. Higher → fewer sites (can collapse yield).
force_width3–15
Fixed width each site is normalised to — the RBP footprint (~9 nt).
cluster_gap_width4–16
Max gap when clustering adjacent sites into a region.
…7 knobs total
4 PureCLIP + 3 post-processing, all within fixed bounds.

The Score — what "good" means

The composite — the single number the optimizer climbs
composite = (0.5·reproducibility + 0.25·motif + 0.25·recall) × collapse guard
reproducibility ×0.500–1
Replicate agreement corrected for chance: (observed−expected)/(1−expected). Can't be faked by keeping a few broad sites.
motif ×0.250–1
Fraction of sites carrying the RBP's expected RNA motif (log-odds PWM). Biological validity.
recall ×0.250–1
Fraction of known-strong ENCODE reference regions recovered. Anchors to ground truth; penalises collapse.
collapse guardmin(1, n/10)
Scales the score down below ~10 sites. With a handful of sites, rep & motif trivially hit 1.0 — this stops that "win".

Also shown: motif_enrichment (× over shuffled background; >1× = real) · n_binding_sites (watch for collapse).

⚠️ The "0.775" Trap

Highest logged score

0.775

QKI_K562 · Optuna · iter 13

rep 1.000 · motif 1.000 · recall 0.10 · n_sites = 1
  • One site trivially agrees with itself & sits on a motif.
  • The logged value skipped the guard; guard-adjusted ≈ 0.078 (and the live dashboard already shows it that way).
  • Same failure twice more — all Optuna: QKI_HepG2 0.554 (n=6), PUM1 0.361 (n=9).

Lesson: this is why the collapse guard exists — and why you always check n_binding_sites next to the score.

Why notable: a "sharp-detection, wide-footprint, precision-mode" regime — the opposite of defaults on two switches. The LLM never explored it; Optuna's blind search found it by ignoring the prior.

Biological Trade-offs

The 0.5 weight on reproducibility tempts the optimizer to buy agreement by shrinking the site set.

True optimal centers
  • RBFOX2_HepG2 (0.498): recall 0.90 + rep 0.46 on 603 sites — breadth and agreement.
  • QKI_HepG2 (0.401): rep 0.39 / motif 0.47 / recall 0.35, n=127 — balanced.
Reproducibility-for-recall
  • PUM1_K562 LLM (0.458): rep → 0.72 but recall → 0.0, n=18 — near-collapse (but the LLM saw it).
  • The § collapses: the pathological extreme.

Pattern: on HepG2 it found real centers; on the sparser K562 signal it trades recall for reproducibility — only the guard keeps that honest.

The LLM's Reasoning

Its decision trail (decisions.jsonl, shown live on the Runs page) reasons from the math and biology.

Objective-aware (QKI_HepG2):

"The composite improved … despite a drop in reproducibility … because the weight on reproducibility (0.5) was offset by gains in motif and recall. The drop is acceptable since it is chance-corrected."

Motif-geometry-aware (QKI_K562):

"The known motifs for QKI are ~11nt long … Increasing force_width to 11 might capture more of the full motif span, raising motif_hit_rate."

And it self-polices: "…collapsed n_sites to 18 and recall to 0.0, triggering the collapse guard" — it declined the degenerate win.

PART 6

Takeaways

Interpretation & Next Steps

  1. The loop delivers real gains on every dataset — biggest where defaults did worst.
  2. LLM = reliable, Optuna = explorer. LLM never collapsed a dataset; Optuna found the best single config but needs the guard to stay honest.
  3. Always read score with n_binding_sites. The dashboard's live re-scoring enforces this automatically.
  4. RBFOX2_K562 is the open problem — best is still only 0.235; reproducibility stuck at ~0.10.
  5. Harden the objective — IDR-based reproducibility + dinucleotide-shuffled motif background.
One-line takeaway
Real biological gains on every dataset; the LLM is the reliable optimizer, Optuna the stronger explorer — and the collapse guard is what makes the comparison honest.
