\section{Results}
\label{results}

The analysis provides 12 completed dataset--cell-line comparisons between the
prior-equipped LLM and TPE: seven in K562 and five in HepG2. Every reported value
is the best chromosome-21 composite observed in one run and is therefore a point
estimate. The cohort is a subset of the 26-dataset software registry, not an
exhaustive survey of the RBPs that the pipeline can process.

\subsection{Automated search usually improved the shared default}

The fixed default was evaluated as iteration~0 of each LLM run. Among the 12
prior-equipped LLM arms, optimization improved this baseline in nine and tied it
in three. Among the 12 TPE arms, the best sampled configuration exceeded the
corresponding default in 11; SF3B4 HepG2 was lower by 0.006. The largest
default-to-best gains were 0.220 for RBFOX2 HepG2 TPE and 0.151 for U2AF2 K562
TPE. These observations support dataset-specific tuning, although they do not
separate optimizer effects from evaluation budget.

\subsection{TPE had the higher observed score in most pairs}

TPE produced the higher point estimate in six of seven K562 pairs and four of
five HepG2 pairs. The prior-equipped LLM was higher for QKI K562 (0.328 versus
0.294) and SF3B4 HepG2 (0.460 versus 0.454). Across all 12 pairs, TPE was higher
in ten. The largest head-to-head gaps
favored TPE for RBFOX2 HepG2 ($+0.189$), HNRNPK HepG2 ($+0.135$), U2AF2 HepG2
($+0.125$), and U2AF2 K562 ($+0.124$).

This pattern is stronger than in the earlier partial batch, but it still does
not identify a superior optimizer under matched conditions. Completed TPE runs
used 14--16 evaluations, whereas LLM runs used five to seven in the head-to-head
cohort. TPE also had ten startup samples before its stagnation rule applied. The
comparison therefore measures the best result under the implemented stopping
rules, not equal-budget sample efficiency.

\subsection{Biological context had a dataset-dependent effect}

Six K562 datasets have both LLM conditions (Figure~\ref{fig:prior}). The prior
increased the best score for HNRNPK by 0.087 and QKI by 0.009, decreased it for
PUM2 by 0.031, U2AF2 by 0.037, and SRSF1 by 0.004, and made no difference for
SF3B4. For the pre-specified crisp--degenerate--positional gradient, only HNRNPK
benefited; PUM2 and U2AF2 did not. The additional datasets likewise show no
uniform direction. RBP-specific context changed search outcomes, but these
single-run ablations do not establish a class-level effect.

\subsection{Metric decomposition and decision trace}

The best configurations reached similar composite values through different
component profiles (Table~\ref{tab:bio}). U2AF2 K562 TPE combined high reference
recall with 180 sites, whereas QKI K562 LLM obtained stronger motif enrichment
but low reference recall and only 19 sites. RBFOX2 HepG2 TPE produced the largest
selected call set and recovered 90\% of the reference regions. The aggregate
score should therefore be read together with its components.

The best HNRNPK LLM configuration increased replicate reproducibility from 0.273
to 0.435 and motif hit rate from 0.273 to 0.348, while reference recall decreased
from 0.15 to 0.10. Its first proposal invoked HNRNPK's C-rich motif to reduce
bandwidth and simultaneously increased merge distance; that proposal lowered
the score. The next proposal retained the narrower bandwidth, restored merge
distance, and increased the minimum crosslink count, reaching 0.329. The trace
shows that the prior influenced the rationale, but simultaneous parameter
changes prevent a causal attribution.

An exploratory inspection of development traces found that readable LLM
rationales were not always directionally consistent with the score components.
Additional prompt detail did not remove this behavior in the small inspected
sample, and target-gene information could not be evaluated because the feedback
contained no peak-to-gene annotations. These observations motivate a controlled
prompt study but do not support a comparison between prompt variants.
