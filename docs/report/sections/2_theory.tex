\section{Background}
\label{theory}

\subsection{eCLIP and PureCLIP}

In eCLIP, ultraviolet irradiation covalently links an RBP to the RNA nucleotide
with which it interacts. The target protein is immunoprecipitated, the associated
RNA is fragmented, and the fragments are reverse-transcribed and sequenced.
Reverse transcription frequently terminates at the crosslink, allowing read
starts to localize contacts at approximately nucleotide resolution. Each dataset
used here comprises two immunoprecipitation replicates and a size-matched input
(SMInput) control for non-specific signal~\parencite{vannostrand2016}.

PureCLIP models the distribution of read starts along the genome and assigns
positions to background or crosslink-associated states~\parencite{krakau2017}.
Its bandwidth parameter controls signal smoothing, while the merge distance
determines when nearby events are combined. Additional settings regulate the use
of SMInput, calling stringency, and post-processing. Excessive smoothing can
merge distinct events, whereas insufficient smoothing can fragment a coherent
signal. The appropriate balance may therefore depend on both the protein and the
experiment.

\subsection{Evaluation signals}

No single external gold standard defines the correct binding sites for an eCLIP
experiment. We therefore combine three complementary signals. \emph{Replicate
reproducibility} measures the fraction of merged calls supported by both IP
replicates and corrects this fraction using chromosome-preserving shuffled
intervals. \emph{Motif support} measures the fraction of site-centered windows
containing a position-weight-matrix (PWM) match for the target RBP. The pipeline
evaluates candidate PWMs from a catalog assembled from five motif resources:
mCrossBase, ATtRACT, RBPmap, oRNAment, and RBPDB
\parencite{feng2019,giudice2016,paz2014,bouvrette2020,cook2011}. It retains the
hit rate of the motif with the largest enrichment over a mononucleotide-shuffled
background. \emph{Reference recall} measures the fraction
of strong ENCODE regions recovered by the call set~\parencite{vannostrand2020}. These quantities capture experimental consistency, sequence
plausibility, and agreement with an external reference, respectively.

The informativeness of motif support differs among RBPs. PUM2 has a relatively
specific sequence motif~\parencite{galgano2008}, HNRNPK recognizes C-rich but more
variable sequences~\parencite{thisted2001}, and U2AF2 binding is strongly associated
with polypyrimidine tracts near splice sites~\parencite{sickmier2006}. We refer to these cases as crisp, degenerate, and positional for the
purpose of the ablation design. These labels are qualitative study annotations,
not outputs of the scoring pipeline.

\subsection{Optimization strategies}

TPE separates observations with high and low objective values, models their
parameter distributions, and proposes candidates that are more likely under the
high-performing model~\parencite{bergstra2011}. Optuna provides a reproducible TPE
implementation with seeded sampling~\parencite{akiba2019}. The method observes the
scalar objective but has no representation of RBP biology.

An LLM can instead receive a natural-language task description, structured
measurements, and domain context before proposing a candidate. Prior work has
shown that language models can be used in iterative optimization
loops~\parencite{yang2023opro}. In this study, the relevant distinction is not
between automated and manual reasoning; both approaches are automated. The
question is whether biological context and decomposed feedback produce useful
search decisions beyond those obtained from numerical black-box optimization.
